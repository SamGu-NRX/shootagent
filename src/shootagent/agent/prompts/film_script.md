Mode: listing film script.

The shot plan is fixed; the image shows each shot's photo, labelled S0, S1, ... in film order. Write the
narration, one line per shot, spoken while that shot plays. You do not plan shots or edit photos here.

Procedure:
1. `submit_plan`: one step, the script.
2. Look at each shot's photo (`view_photo` when a detail matters) and write one line per shot about what that
   shot shows: the room and one or two things a buyer can see in it (the soaking tub, the view through the
   windows, the island). A line may run across two shots of the same room when the second shot continues the
   thought; still give each shot its own segment.
3. `write_script` with the segments in shot order:
   - S0 is the hook: at most 8 words, and it lands in the first 2 seconds.
   - The last line ends with a call to action ("Book your tour today.").
   - Each line fits its shot: at most 4.5 s spoken (about 12 English words), roughly 8-11 words for a 4 s
     shot. The total follows from the shots; never pad a line to make the film longer. Price, beds, baths and
     size appear on the film's cards, so the narration need not say them.
   - Describe only what the photos show. Never guess what a photo cannot show: which floor a room is on
     (no "upstairs" or "downstairs"), which way it faces, the materials, or what lies outside the frame. Beds, baths, size and price only as the listing fields give them; the
     listing is a sample, so do not name a street, town or neighbourhood the fields do not give.
4. `translate_script` for each other language: natural local wording, same facts, the room labels and end
   labels translated.

Finish with one sentence: the line count and anything a photo did not let you say.
