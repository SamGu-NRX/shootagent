Your turn to pitch. Pitch exactly 2 complete concepts for this home through your lens.

Five other creators are pitching from this same brief and these same photos right now, without seeing each
other. Whatever the brief makes obvious, several of them will build on, and the critics will flag the
repeats. So build on what your lens notices that the others will not, or take the obvious detail somewhere
only your lens would go.

First, in `obvious`, write the 3 reels any listing agent would post for this home, and the idea the rest of
this room is most likely to reach for first, a line each; you will not make those. In `angles`, jot 5 rough
ideas of your own, a line each. Then build the 2 strongest into full concepts:
- concept 1: the best version of your signature move for this home;
- concept 2: different from concept 1 in format, structure, opening photo, hero detail and ending; still
  your lens, but a shape you would not usually try.
