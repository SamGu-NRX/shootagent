- uses only the view ids listed in the brief; usually 5 to 12 beats, 12 to 35 seconds of beats in all;
- has a hook that works on mute in the first 1.5 seconds: at most 10 words on screen, over a first photo

- keeps on-screen text to at most 10 words per beat, readable in the beat's time;
- uses voice only if it adds something (the voice is synthetic), at most about 2.6 words per second of the
  beat it plays over;

  buyer knows what is for sale and how much;
- pays off what the hook promised, and ends on purpose (a loop, a question, a punchline, a price reveal)
  chosen for what the viewer should do next;
- sets the music mood and bpm to fit the cuts, and `focus` on the detail for every push, pull or punch;
- lists in `claims` every factual statement it makes about the home: on screen, in voice, on cards and in
  the caption.
