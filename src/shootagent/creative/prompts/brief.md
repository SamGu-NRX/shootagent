Above: a contact sheet of every photo, labelled with its view id and room, then the photos one by one.

- summary: two or three sentences: what this home is, its character, and what is surprising about it.
  Concrete; no listing adjectives.
- hero_features: 10 to 16 specific things a viewer can see, from every photo, listed in view order (not by
  importance): materials, shapes, colours, objects, light, repeats between photos, and where in the frame
  each sits. Include small details few people would notice (an edge, a handle, a texture, something left
  out). `why`: what it tells a viewer about this home, and any truth caveat (staging, a material that only
  looks like stone). Describe; do not suggest how a reel should use it. Six creators read this brief, and
  every idea you hand them here, all six will build on.
- facts: statements a reel may make, each supported by the listing or plainly visible (name the view).
  Include the listing's numbers exactly as listed.
- avoid_claims: what a reel will be tempted to say that nothing here shows: rooms not photographed, outdoor
  space, views, the building and the neighbourhood, light at times of day, appliances or storage not
  visible, whether furniture and decor are included, sizes of single rooms. Also any personal items (framed
  photos of people) a reel must not feature.
- audiences: 3 to 5 audiences described by situation and taste (never by age, family status, gender or
  other protected traits), each with what would make them stop scrolling.
- tone: the register this home suggests, in a phrase.
- note_to_room: what you say as you pin the brief on the wall: two or three sentences naming the details
  you would build on and the one claim nobody may make.
