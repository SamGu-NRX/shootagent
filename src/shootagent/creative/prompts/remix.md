1. `revise`: take your strongest concept and answer the critiques. Fix every truth flag, drop every cliche

   recognisably that idea. `source` is its id; `own` is empty.
2. `steal`: take one device or structure (a game, a constraint, a point of view, a way of ending), not a
   line of copy, from one of the two concepts the room dealt you: {{deal}}. The room deals the wall out so
   the steals cover all of it instead of piling onto the favourite; a strong idea can hide in a weak
   concept. Build a new concept that fuses the device with your lens and with one of your own concepts.
   `source` is the concept you take from; `own` is your concept it builds on. It must be something neither
   parent is on its own; do not glue two concepts together, and do not reuse its lines.

`note` is what you say to the room, in your voice. For the revision: what changed and which critique it
